The Odyssey Metaphor

🗺️ The Map
Your ongoing work visualized as a hand-drawn sea chart. Islands = tasks, storms = blockers, your ship = current focus.
🏛️ Ithaca
Your destination: Q4 goals and career aspirations. Like Odysseus sailing home, you're navigating toward this North Star.
⛵ Your Ship & Crew
Your close collaborators, each with a role (First Mate, Navigator, Quartermaster, Lookout, Deckhands) based on how you work together.
⚓ Islands
Tasks and commitments. Active islands = current work. Stormy islands = overdue/blocked. Sinking islands = forgotten (private).
🌊 The Shipped Continent
Your completed work, growing as you finish tasks. New provinces = recent accomplishments.
🏝️ Calypso's Island
Work you've been stuck on too long. Time to set sail again or let it go.
📜 Captain's Log
Weekly check-in: Athena (AI) summarizes your week, you correct what's wrong. Draft, not verdict.
⚔️ Three Trials
Monthly check-in: Your hardest problems cast as epic monsters (Cyclops, Poseidon, Scylla). You add the story.
📚 Chapters of the Epic
Your months told as mock-epic prose, incorporating your Three Trials stories.
🎁 Gifts from the Gods
Kudos and recognition from teammates via Slack and other sources.
⚓ Ports of Call
Cross-team projects where multiple ships dock together.
🚢 The Armada
Manager view: Your team as a fleet. See who's sailing alone or overloaded (outcomes only).
🦉 Athena's Counsel
Gentle daily nudges from your AI guide (e.g., "Teammate H is waiting on you").
